Gambling, seen from the inside.

Antoine Dugast spent fifteen years on the operator side: at PokerStars from 2010 to 2017, from the opening of the French market to the Spanish launch, then at Betclic, where he led product strategy for the new poker software. He now advises investors as a Third Bridge network expert, and is building a problem-gambling prevention programme for casino staff with the training company SAM.
